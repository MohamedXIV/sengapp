# Community Code of Conduct

Sengapp is about comfortable spaces for real people. The same principle applies to its contributors.

## Be thoughtful

- Treat others with respect, including people with different technical backgrounds and opinions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for newcomers and ask clarifying questions in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not share private messages, identifying information or secrets without consent.
- No harassment, personal attacks, hateful content, threats or spam.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close threads, decline contributions or restrict participation when these expectations are violated.

If an issue concerns conduct, contact the maintainer privately rather than escalating personal disputes in public threads.
